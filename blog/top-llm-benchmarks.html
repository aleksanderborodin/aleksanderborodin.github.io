My top benchmarks

2026-08-11 3 min read Прочитать по-русски →

Which model should you run on 2xV100s with 2x32 GB of VRAM: Gemma-4-26B or Qwen-3.6-35B? Both are modern. Both come from top companies, both are multimodal, and both have polished marketing. Their parameter counts and hardware requirements are similar. But Gemma is completely uncompetitive and measurably worse at every type of task. In cases this clear-cut, benchmarks describe models very well. They help in less obvious cases too, though there is some divergence from reality.

https://artificialanalysis.ai/ - The top option in terms of popularity and how well-balanced the methodology is. It consists of 9 benchmarks and produces 3 indices from them: intelligence, coding, and agentic capabilities. It includes every model and adds them within 2 days of release. OpenAI models are added the same day. It changed its index to show that GPT-5.6 Sol is better than Opus 4.8, but if you use only one benchmark, make it this one.

https://deepswe.datacurve.ai/ - A benchmark of SOTA models on real programming tasks. Its main format plots the percentage of tasks solved against the cost per task. That is even better than cost per token, because some models use more tokens than others. The tasks are exceptionally thoughtful and deep. Right now it is not overcrowded and genuinely shows how useful models are for programming.

https://arena.ai/leaderboard - Like Artificial Analysis, this is not one benchmark but an entire collection. It started by comparing user preferences, but now has many more sections. It has declined in popularity, but remains useful for niche questions such as comparing search quality or image generation.
